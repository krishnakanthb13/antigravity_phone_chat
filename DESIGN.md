# Antigravity Mobile Design System

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The Antigravity Mobile app utilizes a sleek, dark-themed, and glassmorphic aesthetic. The design is intended to feel modern, highly responsive, and "weightless."

## Color Palette & Tokens
- **Background App:** `#0f172a`
- **Background Secondary:** `#1e293b`
- **Foreground Text:** `#f8fafc`
- **Muted Text:** `#94a3b8`
- **Accent/Primary:** `#3b82f6`
- **Border/Divider:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- **Overlay:** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.7)`

## Typography
- **Primary Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Code/Monospace Stack:** `JetBrains Mono, monospace`

## Layout & Mathematical Spacing
All padding, margins, and flexbox gaps adhere to a strict 4px/8px mathematical scale:
- 1 unit = 4px (`gap-1`, `p-1`)
- 2 units = 8px (`gap-2`, `p-2`)
- 3 units = 12px
- 4 units = 16px
- 6 units = 24px

## Components & Atomic Utility Classes
Use utility classes for atoms:
- `.btn-icon`: 24x24 or 32x32 interactive elements with hover states.
- `.card-panel`: Glassmorphic panels with border and subtle shadow.
